ACTIVIDAD 01 · DIÁLOGO

Roller-skating en Venice

🛼

Jane y Tom están patinando en el boardwalk de Venice, California.

🔊 Audio sugerido: assets/audio/unit-2/californian-leisure-venice.mp3

Jane: This is fun! I like roller-skating, but I'm not very good at it.

Tom: Watch out! You're going to hit that man. Oh no!

Jane: Oh! I'm terribly sorry. Are you hurt?

Man: No, that's O.K., honey. I'm all right. Say, are you from England?

Jane: Yes, from London. You see, I'm not used to roller-skating. It's not really an English sport.

Man: I love England. Good old rainy London!

Jane: It doesn't rain all the time, but it's not sunny like California.

Man: And is this your gentleman friend? Is he from England?

Tom: No, I live here.

Man: And are you going to show your lady friend around the most beautiful parts of California?

Tom: Yes, I sure will.

Man: Have a good time! And watch out for the pedestrians!

Tom: Goodbye. Nice meeting you.

Jane: Goodbye.

Idea principal: Jane disfruta patinar sobre ruedas, pero no tiene mucha experiencia. Casi golpea a un peatón, se disculpa y conversa con él sobre Inglaterra y California.

ACTIVIDAD 02 · DIÁLOGO

Planes para California

🏖️

Después de patinar, Jane y Tom hablan de un viaje por la costa hacia San Francisco.

🔊 Audio sugerido: assets/audio/unit-2/californian-leisure-plans.mp3

Jane: So, are you going to show me around California?

Tom: Sure I am. Tomorrow we'll take the coast road to San Francisco. We can just drive along and stop at one of the beaches for a swim.

Jane: It'll be magnificent. You know, Tom, you're a very good guide... Hey! Do you feel like going for a swim now?

Tom: Right now? Sure. But I have to make a phone call first. There's a phone booth over there. Will you wait for me here?

Jane: O.K., don't be long. Maybe I'll run over another pedestrian!

Tom: Jane, um, I'm really sorry. We'll have to put off our plans for a while. I have to go to New Orleans for work.

Jane: New Orleans! When will you come back?

Tom: Soon, I hope. I'm not looking forward to this job.

Idea principal: Tom planea llevar a Jane a San Francisco por la carretera costera, pero una llamada de trabajo lo obliga a aplazar el viaje y salir hacia Nueva Orleans.

ACTIVIDAD 05 · CÓMO SE DICE

Deporte, ocio y tiempo

🎾

1. Deporte y ocio

I go skiing in the Rockies.
Voy a esquiar a las Rocosas.
I play a lot of tennis.
Juego mucho tenis.
Is it safe to dive here?
¿Es seguro bucear aquí?
Can we go backpacking here?
¿Podemos ir de excursión aquí?

2. Hablar del tiempo

It rains a lot in Ireland.
Llueve mucho en Irlanda.
It snows a lot in Scotland.
Nieva mucho en Escocia.
California has a very pleasant climate.
California tiene un clima muy agradable.
Florida gets a lot of sun.
Florida tiene mucho sol.
What's the weather like in California?
¿Cómo está el tiempo en California?
What's the weather forecast for the weekend?
¿Cuál es el pronóstico para el fin de semana?

3. Proponer actividades

Do you feel like going for a swim?
¿Te gustaría ir a nadar?
Would you like to go for a swim?
¿Te gustaría ir a nadar?
How about going for a swim?
¿Qué tal si vamos a nadar?

ACTIVIDAD 06 · CAPACIDADES

Good at, bad at y feel like

🏊

Capacidades

I'm good at riding.
I'm not very good at wind-surfing.
I'm bad at skiing.
You play tennis very well.
I can swim but I can't dive.

Do you feel like...?

Do you want to go for a swim? → Do you feel like going for a swim?

Recuerda: después de feel like usamos un verbo con -ing.

ACTIVIDAD 07 · PLANES Y OBLIGACIONES

Going to, not going to y have to

📅

Going to...

She wants to take tennis lessons soon. → She's going to take tennis lessons.

We want to have a swim soon. → We're going to have a swim.
They want to travel around California soon. → They're going to travel around California.

Not going to...

Is he going to make a phone call? → No, he isn't going to make a phone call.

Have to...

I need some money. Go to the bank. → I have to go to the bank.

Tom has to go to New Orleans for work.

ACTIVIDAD 08 · COSTUMBRES

I'm used to...

🚲

Hablar de costumbres

I often cycle. → I'm used to cycling.

I don't often water-ski. → I'm not used to water-skiing.

Jane dice: I'm not used to roller-skating.

ACTIVIDAD 09 · CULTURA

Los Angeles

🌴

Una ciudad extensa

Los Angeles es una de las grandes áreas urbanas de Estados Unidos y suele asociarse con autopistas, barrios muy diversos, cine, televisión, playas y una cultura marcada por el automóvil.

El texto cultural contrasta distintas zonas y estilos de vida de la ciudad, y menciona problemas ambientales asociados al tráfico y la contaminación. También destaca la importancia histórica de la industria cinematográfica en la región.

Para las personas que visitan California, las playas, el clima, el deporte al aire libre y los trayectos por la costa forman parte de la experiencia turística más conocida.
