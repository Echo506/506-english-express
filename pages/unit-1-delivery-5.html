La entrevista de Barbara Rivers

Escucha el diálogo y practica la pronunciación.

0:00 / 0:00

Barbara: Mr. Van der Wildt, what are your group's connections with the Mafia?

Mr. Van der Wildt: I don't know what you mean... My group, E.G.I., has no connections with the Mafia. That's a lie.

Barbara: Are you sure about that? How do you explain your meeting with Mafia boss Pete Maresca on Monday, April 4, 1988, in Jamaica?

Mr. Van der Wildt: What are you talking about? I don't know that man.

Barbara: Unfortunately, Mr. Van der Wildt, we have a video film of your meeting with Mafia boss Pete Maresca at the Grand Hotel in Kingston, Jamaica. Please watch this, Mr. Van der Wildt.

Barbara: Here, Mr. Maresca is arriving at the hotel in his red limousine. Here you are arriving at the hotel in your black Mercedes. It is your car, isn't it?

Mr. Van der Wildt: Yeah, it's mine.

Barbara: Here, you are talking to Mr. Maresca in the hotel lobby, and there you are getting into his limousine. Can you explain this?

Mr. Van der Wildt: I want to talk to my lawyer.

Barbara: And I'd like to thank you, Mr. Van der Wildt, for a very interesting interview. This is Barbara Rivers reporting for Thirty Minutes.

Idea principal: Barbara muestra un vídeo que vincula a Van der Wildt con Pete Maresca. Cuando se le pide una explicación, Van der Wildt quiere hablar con su abogado.
