ACTIVIDAD 01 · DIÁLOGO

Jane visita una agencia de viajes

🧳

Jane está en una agencia de viajes de Los Ángeles. Quiere visitar Estados Unidos, pero no sabe por dónde empezar.

🔊 Audio sugerido: assets/audio/unit-4/travel-agency-dialogue.mp3

Jane: Good morning. Can you help me?

Travel agent: Yes, certainly.

Jane: I'd like to get away for a while, and see different parts of the United States.

Travel agent: What kind of vacation would you like to take?

Jane: I'm not sure.

Travel agent: We have a variety of trips in every price range. We have a two-week coast-to-coast package to ten cities. If you take that, you'll get to see Denver, Chicago, Nashville, Washington...

Jane: Actually, I'd rather get away from cities.

Travel agent: If you take our Western Wander Week, you'll visit an Indian reservation and you'll be able to watch a Navaho rain dance.

Travel agent: Then, you'll drive on to the Grand Canyon where you'll spend a night in a log cabin. Afterwards, you can hike down the canyon with a qualified guide.

Travel agent: After that, the tour bus will drive you back to Los Angeles, via a ghost town from Gold Rush days. There's an overnight stop in Las Vegas.

Travel agent: The round trip, including accommodation, meals and tax, comes to $998.79.

Jane: That sounds interesting, but do you have anything more... unusual?

Travel agent: If you like outdoor life, you'll love our Alaskan Adventure. First, you'll fly to Anchorage, and then our local guides will take you on a ten-day rafting and camping trip. That's really getting away from it all!

Jane: That's exactly what I want! How much is it?

Travel agent: It's a bargain! It's great value for money at only $1865.

Jane: That's a bit expensive. But it sounds wonderful! I'd like to go as soon as possible.

Idea principal: Jane prefiere unas vacaciones lejos de las ciudades. Finalmente se interesa por una aventura de rafting y campamento en Alaska, aunque considera elevado su precio.

ACTIVIDAD 05 · GRAMÁTICA

Condicional con if

🔀

If she goes, she'll...

When Jane went to the United States, she visited the Grand Canyon. →
If Jane goes to the United States, she'll visit the Grand Canyon.

If she doesn't go, she won't...

If Jane goes to San Francisco, she'll see the Golden Gate Bridge. →
If Jane doesn't go to San Francisco, she won't see the Golden Gate Bridge.

ACTIVIDAD 07 · GRAMÁTICA

Get y can / will be able to

🚀

Diferentes significados de get

To get up: Peter gets up every morning as soon as he hears his alarm clock.
To get away: She wanted to get away, so she went to Barbados for a week.
To get along: Margie and her little sister don't get along.
To get into: I really got into “Gone With the Wind”.
To get bigger: My company has gotten bigger.

Can, could y will be able to

I can speak English. Next year. →
I'll be able to speak English next year.

I can water-ski. Last year. →
I could water-ski last year.

ACTIVIDAD 09 · CULTURA

Vacaciones y parques nacionales

🏞️

Dos semanas de vacaciones

El estadounidense medio dispone de dos semanas de vacaciones al año. Algunas personas viajan por el mundo, mientras otras prefieren conocer su propio país. Entre esos dos extremos aparece una amplia clase media que encuentra muchas posibilidades de viaje dentro de Estados Unidos.

Al planear vacaciones, muchas personas buscan tres elementos: mar, campo o montaña, con una temperatura agradable. Esto ayuda a explicar los viajes de invierno hacia Florida y los desplazamientos estivales hacia Maine o Massachusetts.

Cada región ofrece paisajes distintos: las playas de las Carolinas, California, Oregón y numerosas áreas naturales pueden atraer a turistas y residentes.

National Parks

Theodore Roosevelt impulsó la protección de espacios naturales y contribuyó a popularizar los parques nacionales. Entre los más espectaculares se encuentran Yellowstone, Yosemite, Glacier y el Grand Canyon.

Los visitantes pueden alojarse en moteles entre pinos o acampar en tiendas. Sin embargo, deben respetar las zonas permitidas: el forest ranger puede imponer multas a quienes acampen donde está prohibido.

Los parques permiten disfrutar de montañas, bosques, ríos y fauna, pero también exigen preparación, respeto por la naturaleza y precaución ante animales como los osos.
