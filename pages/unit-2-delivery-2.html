Barbara: Ronald Jordan, you're the president of Texarkana Oil Incorporated. You own 30 percent of the oilfields in Texas, and your company controls 20 percent of the world's total oil production. How much oil does your company produce per day?

Ronald Jordan: Oh, about two million barrels.

Barbara: How many tons is that?

Ronald Jordan: About 300 thousand tons.

Barbara: I believe that's about three times more than your rival, Seadrill Incorporated...

Ronald Jordan: Yes, you're right, but we're bigger than Seadrill and I think we're more advanced in our technology than they are.

Barbara: You're buying out many other companies that are less successful than Texarkana Oil...

Ronald Jordan: Not many. Just a few. We aren't putting all small companies out of business. We're rescuing those enterprises that aren't as dynamic as we are.

Barbara: So Texarkana Oil, in good Texan style, is getting bigger and better.

Ronald Jordan: That's right. Bigger, better and with a brighter future. That's our slogan.

Idea principal: Texarkana Oil produce aproximadamente dos millones de barriles al día. R.J. sostiene que su empresa es más grande, más avanzada y más dinámica que su rival, Seadrill Incorporated.

Actividad 04 · Cómo se dice

Opiniones, negocios y cantidades

1. Dar una opinión

I believe... = Creo que...
I think... = Pienso que...
I don't think... = No creo que...
In my opinion... = En mi opinión...
You're right. = Tiene razón.
You're wrong. = Está equivocado/a.

2. Actividades de negocios

His company owns 30 percent of the oilfields in Texas. = Su empresa posee el 30% de los campos petrolíferos.
His company controls 20 percent of oil production in the world. = Su empresa controla el 20% de la producción.
His company produces oil. = Su empresa produce petróleo.
Her company manufactures shoes. = Su empresa fabrica zapatos.
Texarkana Oil is buying out smaller companies. = Texarkana Oil está comprando empresas más pequeñas.

3. Preguntar por cantidades

How many barrels of oil does your company produce? = ¿Cuántos barriles de petróleo produce su empresa?
How much oil does your company produce? = ¿Qué cantidad de petróleo produce su empresa?
A lot of barrels. = Muchos barriles.
A lot of oil. = Mucho petróleo.
A few barrels / A little oil. = Unos pocos barriles / Un poco de petróleo.

Actividad 05 · Cantidades

How much? How many?

How much? y How many?

How much? se utiliza con sustantivos incontables: oil, traffic, money, time.
How many? se utiliza con sustantivos contables: barrels, cars, businesses.

Ejemplo: R.J.'s company produces a lot of oil. → How much oil does R.J.'s company produce?
Ejemplo: R.J.'s company produces a lot of barrels of oil. → How many barrels of oil does R.J.'s company produce?

A few y A little

A few se usa con plurales contables: a few barrels, a few businesses.
A little se usa con incontables: a little oil, a little traffic, a little money.

Actividad 06 · Comparativos

Bigger, more dynamic y as...as

Comparativos

Para muchos adjetivos cortos se agrega -er: big → bigger, small → smaller, bright → brighter.

Para adjetivos de tres sílabas o más se usa more + adjective + than: more dynamic than, more advanced than y more interesting than.

Ejemplos: Texarkana Oil is bigger than Seadrill. / Texarkana Oil is more successful than Seadrill.

As...as y not as...as

Tom is as dynamic as Barbara.
Paris isn't as big as London.
Bob isn't as dynamic as John.

Actividad 07 · Adverbios

Slow / slowly y good / well

Adjetivo o adverbio

He is a slow driver. → He drives slowly.
She is a good worker. → She works well.

Usa un adjetivo para describir un sustantivo: a fast driver, a bad worker.
Usa un adverbio para describir una acción: drives fast, works badly, speaks quickly.

Actividad 08 · Cultura

Texas: The Lone Star State

Texas

Texas es conocido como The Lone Star State. Su identidad cultural combina influencias indígenas, mexicanas, españolas, estadounidenses y de comunidades procedentes de muchos otros lugares.

El material presenta a Texas como un estado asociado con la cultura del cowboy, el petróleo, la actividad empresarial y ciudades como Dallas, Houston, Austin y San Antonio. También menciona la influencia Tex-Mex, presente en la música, la comida y las tradiciones de la región.

Austin es la capital del estado y también es conocida por su vida universitaria y musical. San Antonio destaca por su centro histórico y su famoso canal urbano, mientras que Houston se relaciona con la industria energética, la tecnología y la exploración espacial.

La frase “Everything is bigger and better in Texas” expresa de forma humorística una idea de orgullo local que aparece en el diálogo de R.J.: bigger, better and with a brighter future.
