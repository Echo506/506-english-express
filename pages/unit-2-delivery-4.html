Unidad 02 · Entrega 04

De compras

Barbara se encuentra con Cindy, una antigua amiga, y ambas van de compras para conseguir un vestido para una fiesta. Aprenderás a hablar de ropa, tallas, colores, apariencia, precios, necesidades y planes futuros.

ACTIVIDAD 01 · DIÁLOGO

En una cafetería

☕

Barbara se encuentra con Cindy, una antigua amiga de high school, en una cafetería de Houston.

🔊 Audio sugerido: assets/audio/unit-2/shopping-coffee-shop.mp3

Barbara: It's so nice to see you again, Cindy. You look exactly the same!

Cindy: So do you! And I love your TV show! I'm so proud of you! Your life in New York must be so exciting! I'd love to come and visit you with Jake and the twins.

Barbara: Oh, yes...

Cindy: Well, we won't come this summer, but maybe in the new year...

Barbara: Oh... great... Let's talk about that later.

Barbara: Now, Cindy, please help me shop. I need a dress to wear to Ronald Jordan's party tomorrow.

Cindy: I'll take you to Westheimer. I love shopping!

Idea principal: Cindy quiere visitar a Barbara en Nueva York con Jake y los gemelos. Barbara necesita comprar un vestido para la fiesta de Ronald Jordan, así que Cindy le ofrece llevarla de compras.

ACTIVIDAD 02 · DIÁLOGO

En una boutique

👗

Barbara y Cindy llegan a una boutique. Barbara se prueba un vestido de seda que está en rebaja.

🔊 Audio sugerido: assets/audio/unit-2/shopping-boutique.mp3

Salesperson: Can I help you, Ma'am?

Barbara: I'm just looking around, thanks.

Salesperson: Certainly, Ma'am. Please go ahead.

Cindy: Look at this dress, Barbara.

Barbara: Yes, that one's nice. What size is it?

Salesperson: It's size 12. You probably need a size 8.

Barbara: Do you have it in other colors?

Salesperson: Yes. It comes in apple green, mustard, bordeaux and navy blue.

Barbara: Can I try the green one?

Salesperson: Certainly, Ma'am. I'll get it for you. The changing-room is right over there.

Barbara: It's too tight.

Cindy: No, it isn't.

Salesperson: It fits her beautifully. It's the fashion. And she has the perfect figure for it.

Cindy: It isn't too tight, Barbara. It's just sexy!

Barbara: Well, I'm not sure... How much is it?

Salesperson: Normally, that dress is $450. It's pure silk. But it's on sale now. It's only $299.98, plus tax.

Cindy: Take it, Barbara. It looks great on you.

Barbara: Oh, what the hell! I don't have anything to wear tomorrow. Can I charge it?

Salesperson: Of course, Ma'am.

Cindy: Now, you come with me, Barbara, and help me choose a winter coat for my trip to New York. I can't wait to come and see you there! Jake and the twins will be so happy! And we can all go shopping together at Bloomingdale's...

Idea principal: Barbara se prueba un vestido verde de seda talla 8. Aunque piensa que está apretado, Cindy y la dependienta dicen que le queda muy bien. Está rebajado de $450 a $299.98, más impuestos.

Gustos, apariencia y necesidades

💬

1. Expresar gustos

I like shopping.
Me gusta ir de compras.
I love driving.
Me gusta mucho conducir.
I adore going to the movies.

2. Hablar de apariencia

ACTIVIDAD 06 · PRONOMBRES

Me, you, him, her, it, them y us

👥

Pronombres objeto

My friend will visit me.
Your friend will visit you.
His friend will visit him.
Her friend will visit her.
Our friend will visit us.
Their friend will visit them.

Ejemplo: I need some help. → Can you help me?

Practique: We need some help. / He needs some help. / They need some help. / She needs some help.

It fits her

This dress fits Barbara beautifully. → This dress fits her beautifully.

Practique: Anne is visiting Margie and Mike. / She will give Sam some money. / Can you please get Jane a glass of water?

ACTIVIDAD 07 · FUTURO

Will y respuestas negativas

📅

Futuro con will

He's calling Mr. Weinberg now. On Wednesday → He'll call Mr. Weinberg on Wednesday.

Practique: They're making an appointment now. Next week. / She's visiting her mother now. This weekend. / We're driving to Missouri now. On Saturday. / I'm on the plane to Cleveland now. At 4:30.

Will she? No, she won't!

Will she get a new dress for the party? → No, she won't get a new dress for the party.

Respuestas negativas: No, it isn't. / No, she won't. / No, they aren't. / No, I don't. / No, he doesn't.

ACTIVIDAD 08 · LET'S Y ENTONACIÓN

Hacer propuestas

🗣️

Let's...

We'll talk about that later. → Let's talk about that now!

Practique: We'll look at that later. / We'll order dinner later. / We'll have dessert later. / We'll do our work later. / We'll go and visit him later.

Entonación

Repita cuidando la musicalidad:

We're so proud of you!
I'm so happy to see you!
It's so nice to see you!
New York must be so exciting!
Paris is so beautiful!
They're so busy!
You must be so proud of them!

ACTIVIDAD 09 · CULTURA

Compras y rebajas

🏷️

Sales y good deals

En las tiendas de Estados Unidos es frecuente ver letreros que dicen Sale. Esta palabra significa que hay productos en rebaja o con descuento.

La lectura explica que los anuncios, los periódicos y la televisión suelen promocionar descuentos durante fechas especiales y fines de semana largos. Una expresión común es good deal, que significa una buena oferta o una ganga.

El texto también presenta la idea de que el servicio al cliente tiene mucha importancia. Un vendedor amable, atento y dispuesto a ayudar puede hacer que la experiencia de compra sea mejor.

En una tienda, es útil preguntar por tallas, colores, precios, impuestos, cambios, descuentos y métodos de pago antes de comprar.
