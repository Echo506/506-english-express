ACTIVIDAD 01 · DIÁLOGO

Bárbara visita al Dr. Klein

🛋️

Bárbara está en Nueva York y ha ido a ver a su sicoterapeuta.

🔊 Audio sugerido: assets/audio/unit-4/in-case-of-emergency-part-1.mp3

Dr. Klein: How are you this week?

Barbara: Terrible! I've had an awful week.

Dr. Klein: Do you want to talk about it?

Barbara: Well, I don't know what's the matter with me. The other day I was taking a shower when the doorbell rang, and I answered the telephone.

Dr. Klein: Yes. And...?

Barbara: And another time I was walking down the street when my boss came up to me, and I couldn't even remember his name!

Dr. Klein: That's not the way to get a promotion, huh?

Barbara: You're not kidding!

Barbara: And yesterday I was having dinner with a friend in a restaurant, and suddenly I got up and started clearing the table!

Dr. Klein: I'm sure the waiter was pleased.

Barbara: That's not the point, Doctor! What's the matter with me?

Idea principal: Bárbara ha tenido una semana terrible y confunde situaciones: contesta el teléfono cuando suena el timbre, olvida el nombre de su jefe y limpia una mesa en un restaurante.

ACTIVIDAD 02 · DIÁLOGO

La tormenta de Nueva Orleans

⛈️
🔊 Audio sugerido: assets/audio/unit-4/in-case-of-emergency-part-2.mp3

Dr. Klein: How long has this been going on?

Barbara: Well, I was O.K. until I went to New Orleans to work on a jazz documentary. I was staying in a house with the film crew.

Barbara: Everything was going fine, then one night, while I was listening to some music, a terrible storm broke out.

Barbara: Then the lights went out, and the cameraman walked in, and I got really angry with him, I don't know why...

Dr. Klein: This is most interesting. Does this cameraman remind you of your father in any way...? And the music, can you remember exactly which piece it was?

Idea principal: Los problemas comenzaron después de que Bárbara viajó a Nueva Orleans para trabajar en un documental de jazz. Durante una tormenta se enfadó con el camarógrafo sin saber por qué.

ACTIVIDAD 06 · GRAMÁTICA

Was doing... when something happened

⏳

Acción en progreso interrumpida

Take a shower. The phone rang. →
I was taking a shower when the phone rang.

Walk down the street. My boss came up to me. →
I was walking down the street when my boss came up to me.

While

We were listening to the radio. The phone rang. →
While we were listening to the radio, the phone rang.

ACTIVIDAD 07 · GRAMÁTICA

Remember y remind

💭

Recordar y hacer recordar

When I see you, I remember your brother. →
You remind me of your brother.

When I listen to that song, I remember you. →
That song reminds me of you.

ACTIVIDAD 08 · GRAMÁTICA

Los usos de go y get

🚶

Usos de go

go on: How long has this situation been going on?
go away: I want to go away on vacation.
go up to: I'll go up to that man and ask him the way.
go out with: I'm going out with an interesting person tonight.
go through: He's going through a very difficult time.

Usos de get

become: He's getting very thin.
arrive: He gets home very late in the evenings.
obtain: I'm getting my visa today.
buy: Can you get me a coffee?
receive: He gets a check from his father every month.

ACTIVIDAD 09 · CULTURA

Forma física y salud mental

🏃

Keep in shape

En Estados Unidos, el ejercicio físico ocupa un lugar importante. La expresión to keep in shape significa mantenerse en forma. Muchas personas van al gimnasio, a piscinas, a canchas de tenis o realizan rutinas de musculación y aerobic.

La alimentación saludable también recibe atención. Frutas, verduras y ensaladas se consideran opciones importantes para el bienestar.

Salud mental

La figura del psicólogo o terapeuta es común en la cultura estadounidense. Un terapeuta puede escuchar, aconsejar y ayudar a una persona a afrontar un periodo difícil.
