ACTIVIDAD 01 · DIÁLOGO

Noticias en la radio

📻

Tom y Mike viajan por una autopista de Los Ángeles. Mike conduce y la radio está encendida.

🔊 Audio sugerido: assets/audio/unit-4/on-the-highway-part-1.mp3

Disc jockey: This is Radio WXQR bringing you the news headlines at one o'clock.

Newscaster: The new defense budget has been rejected by Congress.

Newscaster: One person was killed and six were injured when Hurricane Edie swept over Florida last night.

Newscaster: The Oscar for Best Film was won by “Keep Smiling” directed by Robert Reelberg.

Newscaster: And now, a word from our sponsor:

Teenage girl: I don't know what's wrong with me. I keep losing my friends. I'm never asked for a second date...

Man: If Debbie used Fresh 'n' Kleen underarm deodorant, she would be really popular. The phone would keep on ringing, and she'd never sit home alone!

Idea principal: Las noticias hablan de un presupuesto rechazado, daños provocados por un huracán y un Oscar. Después comienza un anuncio de desodorante que promete popularidad.

ACTIVIDAD 02 · DIÁLOGO

Tom está harto de Los Ángeles

🚗
🔊 Audio sugerido: assets/audio/unit-4/on-the-highway-part-2.mp3

Tom: Switch that thing off, Mike!

Mike: What's the matter with you, Tom? You're very irritable these days.

Tom: Oh, nothing.

Mike: How's Jane?

Tom: I don't know. She went away without saying goodbye.

Mike: Oh, I see... How's work going?

Tom: Not too well. I'm fed up with L.A. I might go somewhere else.

Mike: Where would you go?

Tom: Well, if I left L.A., I'd go to Chicago, or maybe New York.

Mike: If I were you, I wouldn't go. If you moved back East, wouldn't you miss sunny California?

Tom: Hey, Mike, cut it out. Will you do me a favor and stop asking me so many questions?

Mike: Sure, Tom, sure. Do you mind if I switch the radio back on?

Tom: Hey, Mike, would you do me a big favor, and drive me to the airport?

Mike: The airport? What about the Fourth of July barbecue in Santa Monica? Tom, are you feeling O.K.?

Tom: Just fine!

Idea principal: Tom está irritable porque Jane se fue sin despedirse y el trabajo no va bien. Piensa irse de Los Ángeles, pero termina pidiéndole a Mike que lo lleve al aeropuerto.

ACTIVIDAD 06 · GRAMÁTICA

Voz pasiva: was / were done

🔁

Del agente a la acción

The hurricane killed one person. →
One person was killed by the hurricane.

Alfred Hitchcock directed the film. →
The film was directed by Alfred Hitchcock.

ACTIVIDAD 07 · GRAMÁTICA

Voz pasiva: have been done

📰

Presente perfecto pasivo

Congress has rejected the new budget. →
The new budget has been rejected by Congress.

Volunteer doctors and nurses have helped many disaster victims. →
Many disaster victims have been helped by volunteer doctors and nurses.

ACTIVIDAD 08 · GRAMÁTICA

Keep, if y without

🛣️

Acciones repetitivas con keep

I've lost many friends. →
I keep losing my friends.

He's won a lot of prizes. →
He keeps winning prizes.

Situaciones hipotéticas

If I move to Chicago, I'll miss L.A. →
If I moved to Chicago, I'd miss L.A.

Without doing

She went away. She didn't say goodbye. →
She went away without saying goodbye.

ACTIVIDAD 09 · CULTURA

Publicidad

📺

Advertising made in U.S.A.

La publicidad forma una parte importante de la cultura estadounidense. Los anuncios aparecen en radio, televisión, periódicos, revistas, carteles y carreteras. Su objetivo principal es atraer la atención y vender productos.

Muchos periódicos y revistas dependen de los ingresos publicitarios. En televisión, las pausas comerciales son frecuentes y algunos espectadores las aprovechan para descansar, prepararse comida o realizar otras actividades.

Los mensajes publicitarios suelen usar música, eslóganes breves, imágenes de felicidad, referencias a Estados Unidos y símbolos como la bandera. Algunos anuncios pueden ser simples, pero otros emplean humor, creatividad e imágenes atractivas.
