ACTIVIDAD 02 · DIÁLOGO

La llamada a Nueva Orleans

☎️

Barbara llama a Ruth Goldstein para hablar sobre el documental de jazz.

🔊 Audio sugerido: assets/audio/unit-3/appointment-phone-call.mp3

Switchboard operator: L.T.V., good morning!

Barbara: I'd like to speak to Ruth Goldstein, extension 428, please.

Switchboard operator: I'll put you through... I'm sorry, she's on the line. Can I put you on hold?

Barbara: Uh, no. Can I speak to her assistant?

Switchboard operator: Yes. I'll put you through to Kevin Ryan.

Kevin: Kevin Ryan.

Barbara: Hello, Kevin. This is Barbara Rivers speaking. I'm returning Ruth Goldstein's call. She phoned while I was out.

Kevin: Oh, hi, Barbara. Ruth wanted to make an appointment to discuss the jazz documentary. Can you come to our New Orleans office tomorrow at 3 p.m.?

Barbara: Tomorrow?

Kevin: Yes, the cameraman is already here.

Barbara: O.K. Tomorrow at three. See you then. Bye!

Kevin: Goodbye, Barbara. Have a good flight!

Barbara: Thanks.

Idea principal: Ruth está ocupada, así que Barbara habla con Kevin. Acordan una cita al día siguiente a las tres de la tarde en la oficina de Nueva Orleans.

ACTIVIDAD 03 · DIÁLOGO

Un cambio de planes

✈️

Jeff le informa a Barbara que el horario y el alojamiento cambiaron.

Jeff: Hi, Barbara! There's an urgent message...

Barbara: I know. I called back. The meeting's tomorrow afternoon.

Jeff: Yeah. I'm sorry. We changed the schedule.

Barbara: Oh, well. But I wanted to stay in New York at least for a couple of days.

Jeff: Oh! And Barbara... There's something else. We won't stay in a hotel this time. We have a big house for the whole crew.

Barbara: Who's on the crew?

Jeff: There's the cameraman who worked with us on the Van der Wildt interview — Tom Ryder, and...

Barbara: Oh... great!

Idea principal: Barbara quería quedarse unos días en Nueva York, pero debe viajar antes. El equipo no se hospedará en un hotel: vivirá en una casa grande con todo el personal, incluido Tom Ryder.

ACTIVIDAD 07 · GRAMÁTICA

Was y were

🕒

El pasado de to be

I was · You were · He was · She was · It was · We were · They were

Ejemplo: He's in Tokyo. Last week. → He was in Tokyo last week.

Preguntas y respuestas

Was Jane in Europe last summer? → Yes, she was in Europe last summer.

Were the children in the garden yesterday? → No, they weren't in the garden yesterday.

ACTIVIDAD 08 · GRAMÁTICA

Pasado simple, who y while

✍️

Pasado simple

I always return her calls. Yesterday → I returned her call yesterday.

Who

The cameraman is very good. He worked with us yesterday. → The cameraman, who worked with us yesterday, is very good.

While

She called. I was out. → She called while I was out.

ACTIVIDAD 09 · CULTURA

El teléfono en Estados Unidos

☎️

Llamadas telefónicas

En el texto cultural se explican varias posibilidades para realizar llamadas de larga distancia en Estados Unidos: llamadas con operadora, tarjetas telefónicas, llamadas por cobrar y llamadas facturadas a una tercera persona.

Una collect call es una llamada a cobro revertido: la persona que recibe la llamada acepta pagarla. Una person-to-person call permite pedir hablar con una persona específica; si esa persona no está disponible, normalmente no se cobra la llamada.

Aunque las tecnologías actuales han cambiado, estas expresiones ayudan a comprender cómo se organizaban muchas llamadas telefónicas y cómo pedir ayuda a una operadora.
