ACTIVIDAD 01 · DIÁLOGO

Una bebida en The Blue Lagoon

🍸

Tom y Barbara llegan al Blue Lagoon después de la tormenta. Barbara reconoce una cara conocida y recibe atención inesperada.

🔊 Audio sugerido: assets/audio/unit-3/blue-lagoon-drinks.mp3

Tom: Can I get you a drink?

Barbara: No, I'll get these. It's my turn. Another Black Russian?

Tom: Yeah, thanks.

Barbara: Miss! A Black Russian and a double scotch on the rocks, please.

Waitress: Yes, sure. Excuse me, aren't you Barbara Rivers of “Thirty Minutes”?

Barbara: Yes, that's right.

Waitress: Could I have your autograph?

Barbara: Of course.

Waitress: Thank you so much!

Barbara: You're welcome.

Waitress: Wow!

Barbara: Er... the drinks?

Waitress: Oh, sure!

Idea principal: Barbara invita las bebidas. La camarera la reconoce por el programa “Thirty Minutes” y le pide un autógrafo.

ACTIVIDAD 02 · DIÁLOGO

El pasado secreto de Barbara

🎤

Tom entrevista informalmente a Barbara sobre su carrera, hasta que aparece Jack, un antiguo amigo y pianista de jazz.

🔊 Audio sugerido: assets/audio/unit-3/blue-lagoon-secret-past.mp3

Tom: Don't you ever get tired of being famous?

Barbara: You get used to it. Anyway, you're pretty well known yourself in the film world. How long have you been a cameraman?

Tom: Since I moved to L.A.

Barbara: And how long have you lived in L.A.?

Tom: For ten years.

Barbara: What did you do before that?

Tom: Lots of things. I drove a truck, I gave Spanish lessons, I went around the world... Gee! Do you realize you're interviewing me?

Tom: Let me ask you some questions. Have you always been a TV reporter?

Barbara: No, I've done other things too.

Tom: Like what?

Barbara: Oh, it's a long story...

Jack: Hey! Aren't you Barbara Rivers?

Barbara: Jack!

Jack: Where have you been?

Barbara: It's great to see you!

Idea principal: Tom ha sido camarógrafo desde que se mudó a Los Ángeles. Barbara revela que tuvo otras experiencias laborales, pero evita contar su historia completa.

ACTIVIDAD 03 · DIÁLOGO

Un viejo amigo

🎹

Tom: Another fan?

Barbara: Not exactly. Jack's an old friend. He's also a brilliant jazz pianist.

Jack: Sure I'm a fan! Barbara's one of the best jazz singers I've ever worked with. She used to be a singer at “The Happy Hour”...

Barbara: You see, Tom, even TV journalists have their secret pasts...

Tom: Hey! I want to know all about it.

Barbara: Some other time. Let's listen to the music. I love this song...

Idea principal: Jack revela que Barbara solía cantar en The Happy Hour. Barbara prefiere dejar su pasado como cantante para otra ocasión.

ACTIVIDAD 07 · GRAMÁTICA

Presente perfecto

⏳

Acciones que continúan hasta el presente

I have lived in L.A. for ten years.
He has been a cameraman since 1980.
We have worked very hard this year.
She has always loved jazz.

Preguntas con how long, always y ever

How long have you lived in L.A.?
Have you always been a reporter?
Have you ever seen the Grand Canyon?

ACTIVIDAD 08 · GRAMÁTICA

Since, for y ago

🕒

Since y for

I've lived in L.A. since 1977.
They've lived in Pittsburgh for twelve years.

Usa since con un punto de inicio y for con una duración.

Ago y for

I started working as a waitress three weeks ago. → I've worked as a waitress for three weeks.

ACTIVIDAD 09 · CULTURA

Bebidas y bares

🍹

Nombres curiosos de bebidas

En los bares estadounidenses existen bebidas con nombres muy conocidos, como Black Russian, White Russian, Bloody Mary, Tom Collins, Tequila Sunrise, Screwdriver y Long Island Iced Tea.

Un Black Russian suele asociarse con vodka y licor de café. El White Russian añade crema, mientras que un Screwdriver combina vodka y jugo de naranja.

El texto también menciona los “singles bars”, espacios sociales donde adultos pueden conversar, bailar o conocer nuevas personas. Las bebidas alcohólicas son solo para adultos y deben consumirse de forma responsable.
